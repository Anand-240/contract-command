Modules

Four modules covering the full acquisition cycle

Each module writes to the same case record, so procurement, finance and audit teams read one version of the truth.

Contract Management

Hold every clause, amendment and milestone against the parent contract, with expiry and liquidated-damages alerts.

  • Amendments & addenda
  • PBG and warranty expiry
  • Milestone-linked obligations

Procurement Planning

Build the annual acquisition plan, route AoN cases and track indent-to-RFP timelines against sanctioned budget heads.

  • Annual plan & budget heads
  • AoN and RFP tracking
  • Category-wise forecasting

Vendor Evaluation

Score registered vendors on delivery record, quality rejections, indigenous content and financial standing before award.

  • Technical & commercial scoring
  • Past-performance ratings
  • Debarment & blacklist checks

Invoice & Payment Tracking

Match invoices to PO and receipt inspection, run the approval chain and record release with full payment history.

  • Three-way match on receipt
  • Deductions & recoveries
  • Ageing and release status

Governance

Built for accountability and compliance

Procurement decisions are reviewed years after they are taken. ContractCommand records who acted, on what authority and against which document, so any case can be reconstructed exactly as it was decided.

Role-based access

Permissions follow the appointment (indenting officer, CNC member, finance approver, auditor), with delegation limits enforced per financial power.

Complete audit trail

Every create, edit and approval is written append-only with user, timestamp and prior value. Records are exportable for CAG and internal audit.

Approval workflows

Multi-tier sign-off routed by value slab, with recorded remarks, return-for-clarification and no path to skip a mandatory approver.

Secure document tracking

Versioned custody of RFPs, contracts, inspection notes and invoices, each checksummed and tied to the case it belongs to.
